# *Begin Again* (2013)

## **My Favorite Quote**
*“You can tell a lot about a person by what’s on their playlist.”*

---

## **Overview**
**Begin Again** is a 2013 **musical drama film** written and directed by *John Carney*, the same filmmaker of *Once (2007)*.  
**Starring:**
- **Keira Knightley** as *Gretta James*, a singer-songwriter.
- **Mark Ruffalo** as *Dan Mulligan*, a struggling music producer.
- **Adam Levine** as *Dave Kohl*, Gretta’s ex-boyfriend and rising rock star.

---

## **Plot Summary**
Gretta, heartbroken after a breakup, meets Dan by chance in a New York bar.  
Together, they decide to **record an album across the city**, capturing the spirit of New York itself.  
Each song becomes a reflection of **hope**.

---

## **Main Themes**
1. **Redemption** – both Gretta and Dan are trying to rebuild their lives.  
2. **Artistic Freedom** – creating music for passion, not profit.  
3. **New Beginnings** – learning to start over after failure.

---

## **Fun Facts**
- The soundtrack features *“Lost Stars”*, performed by both Keira Knightley and Adam Levine. It was a great hit.
- Most of the outdoor scenes were **filmed live** to capture authentic city sounds.

---

## **Why I Love It**
I love *Begin Again* because it’s not just about making music out of nothing. It’s about **second chances** and finding beauty when **we least expect it**.  
It’s a film that reminds me to **keep moving forward**, even when things don’t go as planned. Finally, I though they would be together in the end. I love it because it went for an unexpected ending!
